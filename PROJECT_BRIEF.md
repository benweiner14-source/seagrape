## What This Is

Seagrape Supply Co. is a bespoke lifestyle apparel studio designing hyper-personalized capsule collections for private country clubs. The business fills a genuine white space: every CC pro shop sells the same performance polos and quarter zips. Nobody sells lifestyle apparel — the thing a member actually wants to wear to the airport, on a boat, or to dinner after a round.

This is not a vendor relationship. It's a creative studio that designs a small seasonal capsule (4-6 pieces) specific to each club's identity, history, and culture. The product is a Comfort Colors heavyweight tee with original illustrated graphics that tell the story of that specific club.

The pilot client is **Admirals Cove Golf & Yacht Club** in Jupiter, Florida.

## The Brand

**Name:** Seagrape Supply Co.

**Why this name:** The seagrape tree (Coccoloba uvifera) is native to South Florida's coastline — gnarled, low-spreading, with round waxy leaves and sparse fruit clusters. It marks the transition from land to shore. The name is hyper-local, completely ownable in the apparel space (confirmed clean — no meaningful US competitor), and carries an old Florida trading post energy that fits the brand perfectly.

**Brand energy:** Old Florida trading post. Utilitarian, worn, not precious. Think: a shop that's been on the Intracoastal since 1952. Not preppy, not streetwear — somewhere between the two. Comfort Colors blanks, original illustrated graphics, hang tags that mean something.

**Comparable brands:** Malbon Golf, Lafarve, Kith. The name shouldn't explain the product. The brand builds a world; golf and boating are the vehicles.

**Brand positioning:** Not a vendor. A creative studio that happens to sell shirts. Each capsule is a limited creative project about a specific club. Members aren't buying merch — they're buying a piece of their club's identity that was made specifically for them.

## The Business Model

**Sales motion:** Approach club GMs or head pros. Present a full bespoke capsule concept — lookbook, mockups, the story — before a single shirt is printed.

**Pricing structure:**
- Design fee: $3,000–$5,000 upfront (covers creative, AI generation, lookbook build)
- Pre-order model: members pre-order through a private URL — only print what's sold, zero inventory risk for the club
- Retail price: $55–$65 per tee
- Your cost per unit: ~$14–16 (blank $8–10 + screen print $4–6)
- Your margin per unit: ~$39–49 at pre-order retail

**What a successful capsule looks like:**
- Admirals Cove has ~500–600 member families
- Conservative 10% buy rate = 55–60 units
- Design fee ($4K) + unit margin (55 × $40 = $2,200) = ~$6,200 per capsule
- 3 drops per club per year = ~$18K per club
- 5 clubs = ~$90K lean operation

**The unfair advantage:** AI design means a full capsule concept can be pitched within days of first contact. Traditional apparel vendors take weeks and charge for design time. Speed is the pitch.

**⚠️ HARD RULE — every graphic prints on the BACK of the tee (see "The Capsule" header).**
Every lifestyle shot must show the **back** of the garment — subject facing away from camera,
a rear three-quarter angle, or looking back over a shoulder. A straight-on front/chest shot
hides the graphic entirely and is not usable, no matter how good the framing otherwise looks.

chest-forward in their original form — pull the *lighting, setting, and unbothered energy* from
